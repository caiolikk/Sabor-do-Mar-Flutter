# Imagens do cardápio

Criadas com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen para este projeto, usando fotografias
sintéticas de comida. Arquivos locais referenciados pelo Flutter:

- `assets/images/salmao.png`
- `assets/images/filadelfia.png`
- `assets/images/hot.png`
- `assets/images/camarao.png`

Prefixo comum dos prompts: “Use case: product-mockup. Asset for Flutter temakeria menu.”

## salmao.png
Wide landscape food photograph of a fresh salmon temaki sushi hand roll, diced raw salmon, rice, cream cheese, scallions, sesame, crisp dark nori, on black ceramic plate on warm wooden table. Temaki fills right half, left half dark negative space for text overlay. Warm appetizing restaurant lighting. No text or lettering.

## filadelfia.png
Landscape close up food photograph of a salmon Philadelphia temaki sushi hand roll, chunks of raw salmon, white cream cheese, rice, scallions and sesame inside dark nori cone, on black ceramic plate, warm wood restaurant background, appetizing, no text.

## hot.png
Landscape close up food photograph of a hot salmon temaki sushi hand roll, cooked shredded salmon with crispy golden topping, cream cheese rice scallions in dark nori cone on black ceramic plate, warm wooden restaurant background, appetizing, no text.

## camarao.png
Landscape close up food photograph of a shrimp temaki sushi hand roll, pink cooked shrimp with white cream cheese rice scallions sesame in dark nori cone on black ceramic plate, warm wooden restaurant background, appetizing, no text.
